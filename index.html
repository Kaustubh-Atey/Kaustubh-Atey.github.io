About

I am a PhD student at the Centre for Machine Intelligence and Data Science (C-MInDS), IIT Bombay. My research studies how machine learning models can remain reliable when the data they see at test time differs from the data they were trained on. I develop methods that generalize across changing data distributions and that can estimate their own uncertainty.
